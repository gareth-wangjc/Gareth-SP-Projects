## Key findings
- **The build differs from the PRD.** The create form uses a Case Type picker, not a Yes/No "Technical Locker Issue" radio. Ticket Center tabs are Team To Do, Team Unassigned, Assigned To me, Related to me, Own by my team and Related To My Team. Team Entry options are Admin, SP, SP Admin and Station. The action link is "Response" and its popup is "Respond".
- **Neither TD is an as-built spec.** One is business background and the other is an early design discussion, so the guide follows the PRD, the UAT sheet and the screenshots.
- **To be Replaced and TBC by the store are TW-only statuses.** Other markets use Created, Assigned, Repairing and Closed (shown as Resolved).

## Decisions made
- **PC only.** SP App (PRD section 6) and the SOPA query API (section 4.6) are out of scope for this guide.
- **No screenshots for filters, TC tabs beyond the list, or most FMS views.** Short text covers them.
- **Reassign shown under Comment/Response Info.** The PRD put it in the Ticket Log, but the build records it in the Respond tab.
- **Nav status "upcoming".** Flip to "live" after go-live.
- **Delete the source branch on merge.** One branch per guide, so later changes get a new branch.
- **Token stays out of memory and files.** Only the env var name is recorded.
