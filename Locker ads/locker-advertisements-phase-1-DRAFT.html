Locker Advertisements (Phase 1)

SPX Inhouse Locker screens can now play advertisements. Phase 1 covers SPX Ops uploading and scheduling internal SPX ads only — seller-bought ad space (Shopee Sellers and Non-Shopee Sellers) is out of scope until later phases. This guide documents the module as built in SP Portal.

Overview

What this feature does

Inhouse Locker screens display advertisements to buyers and drivers while they interact with the locker (the ad exits when the screen is tapped). Phase 1 gives SPX Ops a self-serve way to configure playback settings, group lockers into playlists, and upload/schedule internal SPX ad creatives — replacing the previous manual, backend-driven process.

Everything in this guide lives under SP Portal → Admin → Inhouse Locker Management → Locker Ads (for TW: SP Portal → Admin → Locker Management → Locker Ads), unless stated otherwise.

Phase 1 scope: Internal SPX ads only. Seller-bought ad space (Shopee Sellers via Seller Center, Non-Shopee Sellers via Shipment Portal), spot-based pricing, AI creative review, and billing are not part of this build — see What's not live yet.
Key concepts

How ad delivery works

Three concepts drive everything else in this guide:

CreativeThe ad image itself (JPG/JPEG/PNG, 1080×1720, max 10MB). In Phase 1, every creative belongs to SPX Ops (Company is fixed to "SPX Express").
CampaignOne scheduled run of a creative over a date range — this is what Ops actually creates when uploading an ad. Each campaign gets a system-generated Campaign ID and a status (Scheduled → Live → Completed, or Cancelled at any point before Completed).
PlaylistAn ordered group of campaigns. Each locker is assigned to at most one playlist at a time, and a playlist can be assigned to many lockers. Ops manage playback order within a playlist rather than assigning ads to lockers one by one.

In short: Ops uploads a creative as part of creating a campaign, the campaign is added to one or more playlists, and every locker assigned to that playlist plays it.

Getting started

Accessing the Locker Ads module

Go to SP Portal → Admin → Inhouse Locker Management → Locker Ads. The module has four tabs: Overview (landing tab), Schedule, Ad Management, and Booking Configuration. The + Upload Internal Ad button in the top-right is available from every tab.

Booking Configuration

Setting market-level playback parameters

Under the Booking Configuration tab, Ops set two required playback parameters that apply across the market:

Ad slot durationHow many seconds each ad plays for. Used to estimate total spots for future duration-based campaigns — longer slots mean fewer spots per locker per hour.
Inactivity timeoutHow long the locker screen must sit idle before an ad starts playing. Prevents ads from interrupting an active buyer/driver interaction.
Schedule

Manage playlist: viewing a playlist's schedule

The Schedule tab is split into three sub-tabs: Manage playlist, Playlists, and Locker coverage. Manage playlist is the default view — pick a playlist from the left sidebar (or search by name) to see its calendar and stats.

For the selected playlist, this view shows:

  • A calendar with one bar per campaign, showing its run dates, with a red line marking today
  • Three stat cards: active campaigns today, lockers on this playlist, and campaigns this month
  • A table of the lockers currently on this playlist, with a Change playlist action per row
  • An Edit playback order button, and a View link per campaign that opens its campaign details page
Schedule

Playlists: creating and managing playlists

The Playlists sub-tab is the master list — separate from Manage playlist's single-playlist view. It shows every playlist with its operator, locker count, and campaign count, plus Edit / Delete actions.

  1. Click + New playlist. A popup opens.
  2. Enter a Playlist name (required, up to 255 characters) and an optional Description (up to 500 characters).
  3. Click Create. The playlist is added to the list, ready for lockers and campaigns to be assigned to it.

Locker coverage: assigning lockers to a playlist

Turning on ads for a locker

Before a locker can be assigned to a playlist, ads must be enabled on that locker via SP Management → Point List → Edit → Operation Info, by checking Display Locker Ads under the "Others" point function group.

Assigning the locker to a playlist

The Locker coverage sub-tab lists every locker with ads enabled and its current playlist assignment. Filter by Service Point, Device ID, Assigned playlist, or Display locker Ads status.

Ad Management

Uploading an internal ad

Click + Upload Internal Ad from any tab to open the upload popup.

  1. Ad name — up to 200 characters.
  2. Campaign period — start and end date.
  3. Creative file — drag and drop, or click to browse. Must be JPG, JPEG, or PNG, exactly 1080×1720, and under 10MB.
  4. Assign to playlists — defaults to all playlists that allow internal ads; click Customize to uncheck specific playlists.
  5. Click Schedule ad. The campaign is created directly in Scheduled status — internal ads skip any review queue.

Ad statuses

ScheduledSet immediately when the campaign is created and added to at least one playlist.
LiveSet the first time the ad successfully plays on a locker screen.
CompletedSet once the campaign's end date passes — even if it never played (Phase 1 does not track spot counts).
CancelledSet when Ops cancels the campaign; requires a reason of at least 15 characters. Removes the ad from all playlists immediately.
Ad Management

Tracking and cancelling campaigns

The Ad Management tab lists every campaign. Filter by Ad name, Campaign ID, or Status.

Clicking View (from Ad Management or from a campaign row in Manage playlist) opens a dedicated Campaign details page — not a sidebar — showing the creative, campaign info, and a full ad tracking history (every Scheduled / Edited / Live / Cancelled event with operator and timestamp).

From this page, Cancel Campaign is always available for Scheduled or Live campaigns (prompts for a cancellation reason, minimum 15 characters). An Edit option is only available while a campaign is still Scheduled — once Live, only Cancel remains.

Roadmap

What's not live yet

The following are out of scope for Phase 1, per the TD's non-scope list:

  • Sellers (Shopee Sellers or Non-Shopee Sellers) buying ad space themselves — Phase 2 and Phase 3
  • Spot-based pricing/packages and rate cards in Finance Portal
  • AI review of submitted creatives
  • Location-type and region-based targeting/eligibility criteria for playlists
  • Billing and post-campaign stats for sellers
  • Video ad creatives (Phase 1 supports static JPG/PNG only)
